# Data licence

## Our compiled data and results — CC BY 4.0

The datasets compiled by the Tax Justice Network for this study are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are:
- the table of CRS exchange relationships by financial centre, partner and year;
- the coverage, structure and owner-identification inputs;
- the FATCA partner list;
- the members of the negotiation blocs;
- the researched top personal income tax rates;
- the owner × financial centre table built from FGMZ's code;
- the country list;
- the results in `results/`.

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, provided you give appropriate credit:

> Tax Justice Network (2026), *The State of Tax Justice 2026*, chapter 4 (offshore wealth): replication package.

## Third-party data — their own terms

Files from third-party sources remain subject to the terms of their original providers, listed file by file in `data/raw/README.md`. These sources are the BIS, the OECD, the World Bank, the EU Tax Observatory and KPMG; the list of FATCA partners from the IRS is in the public domain. They are redistributed here only for replication. Nothing in this repository relicenses them.
